## Overview

<!-- manual:start -->
A dark-first music and podcast player built on Material 3 names. The brand is a neon lime (`#D7FF00`, shown in Dark; Light uses the olive tone `#535C00`), set against near-black surfaces and white content. Titles are condensed bold caps (Roboto Condensed Bold), body text is Roboto. Screens are led by square artwork cards in horizontal rails, and genre tiles use bold category colors (Light tone 40, Dark tone 80).

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep contrast at least 4.5:1 for text and 3.0:1 for UI boundaries, in every color mode.
- Do make touch targets at least 48px.
- Do bind every color, spacing, radius and type value to a token or style.
- Do use Radio for 2 to 6 options and Select for 7 or more.
- Don't create a component when one in the table covers the need; compose from lower tiers instead.
- Don't hardcode hex, px or font values, and don't detach instances.

<!-- manual:start -->
- The source app wins over Material 3: match its sizes (Search Bar 42, Bottom Navigation 58, Mini Player 56, Switch 44 x 24) and keep the 48 dp hit area.
- Dark is the design reference; Light mirrors the exact same layout. Pin the Semantic mode on the screen frame.
- Screen titles and genre labels are caps in `M3/headline_small_emphasized`.
- Play is an Inverse Icon Button (64 dp in Playback Controls, 40 dp in the Mini Player); the icon fills the container minus its padding.
- Explicit content is Badge Type = Explicit, never the red `error` color. Arabic song text keeps right-to-left alignment; the UI stays left to right.
- Do not bind Size tokens to Button or Icon Button heights (resized instances break).
<!-- manual:end -->
